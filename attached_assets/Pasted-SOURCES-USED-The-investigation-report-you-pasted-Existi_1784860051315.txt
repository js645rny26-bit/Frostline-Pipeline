Verdict

Build the scraper, but do not wire prop_confirmation_score into CORE authorization yet.

The collection layer is useful. The proposed interpretation layer is not sufficiently commissioned.

What is solid

The module has a durable operational purpose:

1. capture available Hard Rock starter and hitter props
2. normalize them to player/game IDs
3. preserve a timestamped pregame market snapshot
4. compare prop-market expectations with Frostline’s independent game projection
5. support historical replay and disagreement analysis

That is not clutter. It has a recurring producer, consumer, and audit purpose.

What needs correction

1. A high strikeout line does not automatically confirm an under

A high K line may reflect:

* pitcher dominance
* opponent strikeout vulnerability
* expected workload
* favorable umpire or park context
* market pricing

But it does not independently establish low scoring.

A starter can clear a high strikeout line while allowing:

* home runs
* walks
* concentrated damage
* early runs before settling
* bullpen exposure after six innings

Use the strikeout market as a starter-shape comparison, not an automatic suppression vote.

2. Earned-runs lines are more directly relevant, but still market evidence

The two starter ER lines can imply a rough starter-run expectation, but they do not account cleanly for:

* unearned runs
* bullpen scoring
* opener/bulk ambiguity
* hook timing
* inherited runners
* game-state changes

ER props are useful comparative truth, not a replacement for the run-band model.

3. Aggregating batter total-base lines is not a clean projected team total

Total-base props are not additive team-run forecasts.

They are affected by:

* different plate-appearance expectations
* juice and bookmaker margin
* missing bottom-order markets
* correlated lineup outcomes
* unequal thresholds
* players without posted lines
* power without run conversion

A lineup with several strong TB lines may signal damage access, but summing those lines would create false precision.

Correct Frostline placement

The layer should sit here:

price-blind projection → Frostline game truth → run bands → vehicle ranking → prop-market comparison → market execution

It should not sit before projection or create the thesis.

For the first implementation, produce only comparative fields such as:

* Starter_K_Market_Signal
* Starter_ER_Market_Signal
* Lineup_TB_Coverage_Pct
* Prop_Market_Direction
* Prop_Market_Agreement
* Prop_Market_Disagreement_Reason
* Prop_Snapshot_TS

Use categorical outputs:

* AGREES
* MIXED
* CONTRADICTS
* INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE

Do not generate a composite authorization score yet.

Workbook discipline

Do not add a new tab merely because the scraper exists.

The raw data belongs in an existing input/history surface only if that surface can hold player-level prop snapshots without mixing responsibilities. Otherwise, a dedicated prop-history tab is justified only when all three are true:

* the scraper writes to it every publish
* a comparison module reads from it
* replay and audit use it later

The board should receive only the summarized comparison fields, not hundreds of raw prop rows.

Commissioning sequence

1. Build scraper and normalization.
2. Confirm IDs, names, teams, books, lines, odds, timestamps, and game matching.
3. Smoke-test opener, scratched starter, missing prop, duplicate player, and postponed-game cases.
4. Run in shadow mode with no CORE impact.
5. Replay historical slates to determine whether agreement or disagreement improves calibration.
6. Only then decide whether it can downgrade stability, veto a vehicle, or remain informational.
