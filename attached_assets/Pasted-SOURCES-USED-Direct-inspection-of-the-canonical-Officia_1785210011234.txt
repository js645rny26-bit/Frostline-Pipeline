What is currently working

Team-level recent offense

The projection engine is receiving:

* Fangraphs aggregate L30 wRC+, converted into a runs-per-nine estimate
* Actual L10 runs scored per game
* A blended offensive rate using approximately:
    * 65% L30
    * 35% L10

Every current game shows Offense_Source_Status = BLENDED.

So this path is real:

Aggregate team L30 offense
+ actual team L10 scoring
→ Offense_Rate_Used
→ game projection

That is not fake or empty.

4. The required team split system is not represented

Our required offense split layer is:

Season vs RHP/LHP
L30 vs RHP/LHP
L14 vs RHP/LHP

The canonical workbook currently exposes:

* aggregate L30 offense
* aggregate L10 scoring
* L10 wOBA
* a lineup platoon-advantage percentage

It does not expose:

* Season team wRC+ versus RHP
* Season team wRC+ versus LHP
* L30 team wRC+ versus RHP
* L30 team wRC+ versus LHP
* L14 team wRC+ versus RHP
* L14 team wRC+ versus LHP

The Away_Platoon_Adv and Home_Platoon_Adv fields only indicate the share of hitters holding the platoon advantage. They do not tell us whether those hitters or that team are actually productive against that handedness.

So the system currently knows something like:

Six of nine hitters have the handedness edge.

It does not reliably know:

This exact lineup has a 121 wRC+ against right-handed pitching over the last 30 days.

That is a substantial distinction.

Why this matters to the current failures

This missing data can directly contribute to the projection errors we just saw.

The engine is currently combining:

aggregate recent team offense
+ starter FIP-derived quality
+ park/weather
+ basic lineup factor

But it is not properly calculating:

exact confirmed lineup
× opposing pitcher handedness
× Season/L30/L14 split strength
× individual contact/damage profiles

That creates conditions for Starter Classification Collapse:

* a weak starter factor can heavily inflate a team projection
* the engine lacks enough exact lineup-matchup information to restrain it
* recent aggregate offense is translated into expected runs without sufficiently proving matchup-specific contact and conversion

ARI–PIT and TOR–WSN are consistent with that vulnerability.
